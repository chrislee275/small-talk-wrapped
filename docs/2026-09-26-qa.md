## Browser environment and scenarios

Browser skill/plugin was not available, so the candidate uses its own Playwright Chromium workflow. Chromium 145 on macOS, viewport sizes 320 × 844, 360 × 844, 390 × 844 and 1280 × 900.

Verified:

- All three full routes, correct personal branch IDs, no horizontal overflow in the route checks, no page-level JavaScript errors in the full-route run.
- No default selection; keyboard focus + Space selects a native radio; Back and refresh behave as documented.
- Changing character clears selection and goes to S23 after selection. Back into a previous session returns to the safe opening rather than restoring an old branch.
- Three local downloads: valid PNG signature and 1080 × 1920 dimensions, names tied to the selected fictional character.
- Paper, checklist and Restart actions reveal their outcome; achievement details remain absent until unlock completes.
- S05 waits until its nameplate reaches the reading area, pauses when scrolled away and stops without navigating after its last name.
- Quantity reveal presents three seed packs, then the final count. Ending scrolls, can pause/resume, stops at the top and does not turn a page.
- Reduced-motion ending displays directly. Show All provides a waiting-free alternative throughout.
- 200% root text sizing on the long chat screen at all three narrow widths, no horizontal overflow and the Next control remains reachable.
- Broken avatar requests display a text fallback; normal avatars appear inside the circular frame.
- A wheel gesture reads long content first; a separate sustained gesture at the bottom advances.

## Limitations — not certified

- No physical iPhone/Android, Safari, screen reader or real touch-device run was performed. Gesture policy has unit tests; wheel behavior has browser coverage.
- Text resizing was tested through root font sizing, not every browser/OS zoom combination or every one of the 48 records.
- No formal WCAG audit, exhaustive color-contrast audit or live-network performance benchmark was performed.
- Privacy checks reduce accidental exposure; they are not a legal/rightsholder review or a guarantee against every possible identifying coincidence.
- The pinned vinext version is beta. npm reports the pinned ESLint 9 release as out of support. Dependency modernization and vulnerability review should be considered before a later public release; neither warning is represented as a runtime failure.
- vinext reports the root route as statically unclassified during build. Production navigation was verified separately.
- Test terminal color-environment warnings are tooling messages, not browser application errors.

The public source scanner intentionally excludes dependency directories, development caches and ignored test artifacts, but scans production build text and committed history. The private-material comparison was performed locally; the private reference corpus and comparison inputs are not part of this repository.
